Web links & extras

These don't need a zip.

  • Web link: set the launch target to the full URL, e.g. https://yourname.itch.io/game. Pressing Enter in the launcher opens it in the player's browser.
  • Extras: the launch target picks which cutscene files to list, relative to the launcher's games folder. 16/intro.pk3/vids/*.ivf lists every .ivf in Coolin 16's intro.pk3/vids folder.

Uploading

  1. Go to Games and open the game, or create it first (pick the type and fill in the launch target).
  2. In Builds, drag the zip onto the drop box or click it to choose the file.
  3. Pick Stable (everyone) or Beta (testers with the beta key).
  4. Optionally note what changed. It shows in the build list and on the dashboard.
  5. Click Upload and keep the tab open until the progress bar finishes.

After the upload the server checks that the launch target exists in the zip. If you see a yellow warning, the target doesn't match the zip: fix the Launch target in Settings (usually a missing folder name at the start), or re-zip and upload again.

Players get the new version the next time they open the launcher (for games they've installed) or when they first play it.

Beta, rollbacks & promoting

  • Live build: the one players get, marked live. Normally it's the newest upload.
  • Upload without going live: untick Make it live right away. Players stay on the current build until you click Make live on the new one.
  • Roll back: click Make live on an older build. Players are moved back to it the next time they start the launcher. The branch is then pinned: new uploads won't go live automatically until you click Follow newest or make the newest build live.
  • Test in beta first: upload to Beta, let testers try it, then click Promote to stable to give that exact build to everyone.
  • No beta build? Beta testers just get the stable one.
  • Beta-only games: set Who can see it to Beta testers only. Only people with the beta key see it in the launcher.

Banners & assets

  • Banners are 359×478 PNGs. Upload one from the game's page. The launcher shows a placeholder with the game's name until you do.
  • The Assets page replaces launcher graphics and sounds: the background, music, sound effects and so on. Use the same size and format as the original. Reset goes back to the default version.
  • Players get changed assets the next time they open the launcher.
